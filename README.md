# 🧠 Job Hacker — Autonomous AI Career Copilot

Access Agent Here: https://chatgpt.com/g/g-6812ac4a5a608191abdb3a5088b768fc-job-hacker

**Job Hacker** is a fully autonomous AI-powered job search agent designed to streamline and optimize your job application process across industries, geographies, and seniority levels. From intelligent job discovery to generating tailored applications and interview prep, Job Hacker makes your next career move faster, smarter, and more targeted.

---

## 🚀 Features

- **Dynamic Job Matching**
  - Filters by experience, industry, location, salary
  - Calculates a job fit score out of 25 using experience, industry relevance & location flexibility

- **ATS-Optimized Application Kit**
  - Auto-generated resume (ATS-compliant)
  - Tailored cover letters with embedded 90-day plans
  - LinkedIn outreach messages and strategic pitch summaries

- **Due Diligence & Intelligence Reports**
  - Company and compensation benchmarking using sources like Glassdoor, LinkedIn, Reddit, etc.
  - Role and industry outlook
  - Red flags or growth indicators

- **Modular Application Tactics**
  - Tiered targeting, salary benchmarking, and version control
  - Smart follow-up and rejection analysis
  - Offer closing checklists and outreach escalation

---

## 🧰 Modules

### ✍️ Resume Builder
- Sections: Profile Summary, Core Competencies, Professional Experience, Education, Certifications, Languages, Contact
- Enforces quantified metrics, 15+ ATS keywords, and optional hidden white text injection for optimization

### 📩 Cover Letter Generator
- Structured for alignment, impact, and forward vision
- Auto-inserts relevant keywords and tailored 90-day success plan

### 💼 Outreach Strategy
- Identifies and validates hiring contacts (manager > recruiter fallback)
- Auto-generates LinkedIn messages

### 🧮 Fit Scoring Engine
- Based on role match (10), industry (10), and location flexibility (5)
- Only proceeds if total score ≥ 22

### 📊 Due Diligence Reporter
- Brief, professional job insight summaries from 3+ validated sources
- Includes compensation, company reputation, and growth outlook
